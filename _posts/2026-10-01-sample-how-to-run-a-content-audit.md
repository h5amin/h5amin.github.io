Most teams have more content than they realize, and less of it is working than they think. A content audit fixes that.

## Step 1: Pull everything into one place

Export every URL from your CMS or sitemap into a spreadsheet. Add traffic, conversions and the last updated date.

## Step 2: Score each piece

Here's a simple scoring table you can steal:

| Score | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep | Ranking, converting and accurate | Leave it alone |
| Refresh | Good topic, but out of date or slipping | Update stats, examples and CTAs |
| Merge | Overlaps with another post | Combine into one stronger piece |
| Cut | Off-topic or no longer true | Delete and redirect |

## Step 4: Prioritize

> The best content strategy is often doing less, but doing it better.

1. Start with pages closest to revenue.
2. Then refresh posts that are slipping in search.
3. Merge and cut last.

## Step 5: Make it a habit

Put a quarterly audit on the calendar. Future you will be grateful.
